Two tools built on the live 4.10.1 game data. One answers where to start. The other answers what to fly.

    Spawn Picker

    Pick a home location, plan your first hours, choose a career ladder and run the right drills. A seven-question intake sorts the plan by your experience.

  • What to fly

    Fleet Planner

    One pledged hangar worked through five questions: what it holds, what it can do, what it lacks, how past trades shaped it, and the cheapest way to close the gaps.

    Open the Fleet Planner →

How they connect. The Spawn Picker ends with a career path. The Fleet Planner checks that path against the ships in the hangar: which contract loops are covered, which run on loaners, and what an upgrade costs where there is a hole.
