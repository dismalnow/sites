      How to melt a pledge, step by step
      1. Sign in at robertsspaceindustries.com and open My Hangar. Multi-factor authentication must be switched on for the account.
      2. Find the pledge by the name shown in the table and expand it.
      3. Choose Exchange. That is RSI's label for melting a pledge into store credit.
      4. The dialog states the credit you will receive. Compare it with the figure in the table. The figures here are estimates from your order history, so the dialog is the authority.
      5. If the amount is right, confirm with your password. The credit lands on the account at once.
      6. A melted pledge moves to your buy-back list. Getting it back costs its original price in new money, or in store credit with the quarterly token.
      How an upgrade works on the RSI site, step by step
      1. Sign in at robertsspaceindustries.com. Open Store, then Ship Upgrades.
      2. Pick the ship you own as the starting ship and the target as the destination. The tool only offers targets that cost more than the starting ship.
      3. Check the price. It should equal the figure on the move below. If it does not, a store price changed since 8 October. Stop and re-check before paying.
      4. Add it to the cart and pay. A standard upgrade accepts store credit. A warbond upgrade needs new money.
      5. The upgrade now sits in My Hangar as its own item. Nothing has changed yet, and you can still melt it for what you paid.
      6. To commit, open the upgrade in My Hangar, choose Apply Upgrade, pick the pledge, and confirm. This step is permanent.
      7. The pledge keeps its name, insurance and extras. Only the ship inside changes.

      Button labels on the site may differ slightly. The order of steps does not.
